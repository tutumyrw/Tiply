Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Tiply ("this site") is a free tip calculator. This policy explains what information is, and is not, collected when you use it.

Information We Do Not Collect

All calculations (bill amount, tip percentage, number of guests) happen directly in your browser. This data is never sent to, stored on, or processed by any server — it disappears the moment you close or refresh the page.

Cookies & Advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendor cookies used for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

Children's Privacy

This site does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. Since the calculator itself stores no data at all, there is nothing collected from any visitor, regardless of age.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes in how the site works or in advertising practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact information listed on the site's GitHub repository.
